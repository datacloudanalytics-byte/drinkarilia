ARILIA 1.28 — OUR STORY PAGE

The Our Story page is rebuilt on the structure of POM Wonderful's Our Story
page: hero with the first flavour, "Where it started" origin story,
"Fruit to bottle" four-step process, a headline-numbers band (7 flavours,
250 ml, 1 bottle, 0 guesswork), "More than meets the label" bottle details,
a "Made for every table" photo gallery, three values and the Stock Arilia
call to action. Copy lives in inc/about.php. Saved Our Story pages receive
the layout at render time; new starter imports get editable core blocks.

Review before launch: the origin copy says Arilia is made by Pionory
Beverages (from the contact email domain) and describes pomegranate as the
first flavour. Add a founding year or hometown if you want them shown.
No nutrition or health claims are made on this page.

Install: Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload Theme > Replace current
with uploaded. Clear GoDaddy cache. Do not recreate pages.
